Quantum amplitude estimation challenge

Every algorithm estimates a = sin θ ∼ U(0.1, 0.9) from Grover-depth-n measurements with p = cos²((2n+1)θ). The score is a constant: the error percentile times the resources used. Lower is better, and the frontier is what counts, not a single number.

How to read the frontier

An entry is Pareto-optimal in a frontier when no other entry is at least as good on all three percentiles and strictly better on one. A method can win on tails (C99) while losing on the bulk (C68), so a single rank hides most of the story.

Why C̃ and not ε · depth: multiplying every shot count by k divides ε by √k and multiplies queries by k, so ε · depth can be driven down at will while ε · queries goes up. The product ε² · depth · queries does not move, which makes it the honest depth-limited figure of merit (the paper's parallel constant ε95 nmax ≈ 0.21 assumes one shot on the deepest rung).
